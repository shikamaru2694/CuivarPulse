## What's new

- **TV mode.** Switch the player from ♪ to 📺 and the song's music video plays as retro pixel art, in sync with the music, in the panel or fullscreen. Three styles: **Retro** (32-colour game palette), **Arcade** (CRT scanlines) and **Game Boy**. Songs with only a lyric video or a still image keep their artwork. TV mode never runs while a game is open, and does nothing when the panel is closed.
- **Fullscreen.** The new ⛶ button shows the artwork big in Music mode, or the pixel video in TV mode. Any button closes it.
- **Simpler light colours:** Artwork (follows the video in TV mode, like Ambilight), Rainbow (flows faster as the song gets intense) and One colour.
- **Bluetooth headphones:** the lights and rumble wait for the audio delay, so they land on the beat you hear.
- **Build-ups:** the lights swell for two seconds before a big drop.
- **A tidier player:** icon buttons, and a Music / TV switch right under the play button.
- **Settings work properly with the D-pad:** the window opens at the top and scrolls as you move.

## Safer, smaller release

- **Built by GitHub** from the tagged source code, with a signed build provenance attestation and a `SHA256SUMS` file. The ZIP is now **0.5 MB** (was 41 MB): no programs or packed code inside, only source files and the built panel. See *What's in the release* in the README.
- **The music engine is downloaded on first launch** from the official yt-dlp and QuickJS releases and checked against their published SHA-256. yt-dlp is now its readable pure-Python version, run with the system's Python.
- **The engine updates itself:** a quiet check once a day, and straight away when YouTube changes something (the song is retried once). A new version is tried before it's used, and the previous one is kept if anything goes wrong. *Update music engine* in Settings stays as a backup.
- Helper programs get only the environment variables they need, and HTTPS uses your system's own certificate store.
